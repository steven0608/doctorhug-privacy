Doctorhug Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · 中文版本

Doctorhug (“the game”, “we”) is a pretend-play doctor game where children help Dr. Mochi care for friends. It is made by ZenBa for iPhone, iPad and Android. This policy explains what information is involved when your child plays.

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no social features.
  • We don't run servers and never receive your child's data.
  • Ads are set up for children: no personalised ads, no tracking, G-rated only. A one-time purchase removes them.
  • The microphone is off unless a grown-up turns on Echo mode, and recordings never leave the device.

What the game stores

  • Progress and settings (stickers, cures, the hospital, language and sound settings, and the "Today" summary for parents: friends helped, minutes played, words heard, healthy choices) are saved only on the device. Deleting the game deletes them. Parents can also clear them with Parents → Start over.
  • We never ask for a name, email, photo, contacts or location. The game does not use the camera.
  • Dr. Mochi's voice is pre-recorded and built into the app; nothing is downloaded or sent.

Echo mode (microphone)

Echo mode lets Dr. Mochi repeat what a child says in a funny voice. It is off by default and can only be switched on by a grown-up in the Parents area (behind a grown-up question), which is also where the device asks for microphone permission. When the child taps the microphone button:

  • up to 3 seconds of sound are recorded on the device,
  • played back once, faster and higher, in response to that tap,
  • and then deleted immediately. Audio is never saved, never sent anywhere and never turned into text.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and may show ads from Google AdMob. Because the game is made for children, every ad request is marked child-directed (COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent (Europe and the UK). This means:

  • No personalised or interest-based ads and no remarketing.
  • No advertising identifier: the game never asks to track, and the Android Advertising ID permission is removed.
  • Only ads rated for general audiences (G).

To show ads, limit repeats and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information: IP address (approximate location), device and app information, and which ads were shown or tapped. See how Google uses data.

Ads never appear during a treatment. A full-screen ad may appear only after a friend is cured and their story ends, never in the first minutes of play, and at most once every five minutes.

Remove Ads purchase

Parents can remove all ads with a one-time purchase in the Parents area. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see payment details. There are no other purchases, no subscriptions and no coins to buy.

For parents

  • Settings, purchases, Echo mode and links that leave the game are behind a grown-up check.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information about your child has reached us, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: ZenBa · zenbainc@gmail.com

Doctorhug 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 8 日

Doctorhug 是一款让孩子帮助麻糬医生照顾朋友们的医生角色扮演游戏，由 ZenBa 制作，适用于 iPhone、iPad 和 Android。

简要说明

  • 没有账号、无需登录、没有聊天和社交功能。
  • 我们不运行服务器，也不会收到孩子的任何数据。
  • 广告已按儿童标准设置：不个性化、不追踪、只显示 G 级。一次性购买即可去除广告。
  • 除非家长开启回声模式，否则不会使用麦克风；录音绝不离开设备。

游戏保存的内容

  • 贴纸、治好的朋友、医院进度、语言和声音设置，以及给家长看的“今天”（治好的朋友、游戏时间、听到的词、健康选择）只保存在设备上。删除游戏即删除这些数据，家长也可以在“家长 → 重新开始”中清除。
  • 我们从不索取姓名、邮箱、照片、通讯录或位置，游戏不使用摄像头。
  • 麻糬医生的声音是预先录制并内置在应用里的，不会下载或发送任何内容。

回声模式（麦克风）

回声模式让麻糬医生用有趣的声音重复孩子说的话。它默认关闭，只能由家长在家长区（需通过家长验证）开启，设备也会在那里请求麦克风权限。孩子点麦克风按钮时：

  • 在设备上录制最多 3 秒的声音，
  • 立即用更快、更高的声音播放一次，
  • 然后立刻删除。录音不会被保存、发送或转换成文字。

广告（Google AdMob）

游戏免费，可能显示 Google AdMob 的广告。所有广告请求都标记为面向儿童（COPPA）及未达同意年龄（欧洲和英国）：不投放个性化广告、不进行再营销、不使用广告标识符、只显示 G 级广告。为投放广告和防止欺诈，Google 仍可能处理有限的技术信息（IP 地址、设备和应用信息、显示或点击了哪些广告）。

治疗过程中绝不出现广告；全屏广告只在朋友治好、故事结束后出现，刚开始玩的几分钟内不会出现，且每 5 分钟最多一次。

去除广告购买

家长可以在家长区一次性购买去除所有广告。付款完全由 Apple 或 Google 处理，我们看不到任何付款信息。没有其他购买、没有订阅、没有可购买的金币。
